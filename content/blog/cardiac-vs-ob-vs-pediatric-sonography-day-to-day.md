Choosing a specialty isn't just about salary. It's about **who you'll be scanning, how fast, and what you'll carry home.** Here's the honest day-to-day of the major paths.

![Cardiac vs OB vs pediatric sonography](/blog/career/specialty-day-to-day.svg "What each specialty is really like|SonoBuddy")

## Cardiac (Echocardiography)

- **Patients:** adults with heart disease, often older and sicker; ICU and ER portables
- **Pace:** steady outpatient echoes plus urgent inpatient studies
- **Skills:** cardiac anatomy, hemodynamics, heavy Doppler, precise measurements
- **The hard part:** scanning critically ill patients in cramped rooms; physical strain
- **The best part:** high clinical impact, and strong demand for cardiac skills
- **Credentials:** RDCS (ARDMS) or RCS (CCI)

## OB/GYN

- **Patients:** expectant families, plus gynecologic patients
- **Pace:** busy schedules, anatomy scans that take time
- **Skills:** fetal anatomy, biometry, placenta and cervix assessment, transvaginal technique
- **The hard part:** the emotional swings. Joyful scans and devastating news can fall on the same day, and families want sex reveals, "cute" pictures, and answers you can't give.
- **The best part:** being part of families' biggest moments
- **Credentials:** RDMS with OB/GYN specialty

## Pediatric

- **Patients:** babies to teens, plus their worried parents
- **Pace:** slower; kids need time, distraction and patience
- **Skills:** pediatric anatomy (pyloric stenosis, intussusception, hips, neonatal heads), creativity to keep a child still
- **The hard part:** sick children and anxious families
- **The best part:** often described as the most rewarding work in imaging
- **Path:** see [Pediatric Sonographer Requirements](/blog/pediatric-sonographer-requirements)

## Vascular

- **Patients:** DVT checks, carotids, arterial disease, dialysis access, vein mapping
- **Pace:** long, detailed Doppler exams
- **Skills:** physics and Doppler mastery, velocity criteria, hemodynamics
- **The hard part:** physically demanding, long exams; precision matters
- **The best part:** very technical; strong demand for RVT-credentialed sonographers
- **Credentials:** RVT (ARDMS) or RVS (CCI)

## General (Abdomen + Small Parts + Some OB)

- **Patients:** everyone: RUQ pain, kidneys, thyroid, scrotal, appendix
- **Pace:** fast and varied, especially in hospitals with ER coverage
- **The best part:** never boring, and very employable everywhere
- **Credentials:** RDMS (abdomen, often plus OB/GYN)

## How to Choose

Ask yourself:

1. **Which patients energize you?** Babies, families, the critically ill, kids?
2. **How do you handle emotional weight?** OB and peds carry more of it.
3. **Do you love physics and Doppler?** Vascular and cardiac reward it.
4. **Do you want variety or depth?** General gives variety; specialties give depth.
5. **What does your local job market need?** Check real openings in your area.

For pay by specialty, see [Highest Paid Sonography Specialties](/blog/highest-paid-sonography-specialties). For every credential path, see [Sonographer Specializations: Complete Guide](/blog/sonographer-specializations-complete-guide).

## The Bottom Line

There's no "best" specialty, only the one whose **patients, pace, and pressure** fit you. Shadow at least two before you decide.
